optimalTime: O(n)
optimalSpace: O(1)
pitfalls:
  - Incrementing `mid` after swapping with `high`, the swapped-in value is unprocessed and must be checked again.
  - Using `mid < high` instead of `mid <= high`, which can leave a 2 at `high` unsorted.
