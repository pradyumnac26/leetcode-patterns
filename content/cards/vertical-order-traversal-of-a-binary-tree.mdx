After traversal, iterate through the columns after sorting the keys of the dictionary. For each column, sort its `(row, value)` pairs so nodes are ordered first by row and then by value when rows are equal. Extract the values from each sorted column and append them to the final result."
optimalTime: O(n log n)
optimalSpace: O(n)

  - Iterating directly over a dictionary does not guarantee columns are processed from smallest column to largest based on their numeric value. Use `sorted(res.keys())`.
